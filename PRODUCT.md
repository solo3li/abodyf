# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students (buyers) and Executors (sellers) participating in a marketplace for various services. They need a reliable and safe environment to connect, request services, and manage orders.

## Product Purpose
To provide a secure and flexible platform connecting students with service executors, facilitating order creation, payment (via Paymob), file exchange, and communication through chat and support tickets.

## Brand Personality
Professional, safe, trusted, flexible.

## Anti-references
Should avoid looking like a cluttered, untrustworthy, or overly complex generic freelance platform. It should not look like a social media app. Avoid dark patterns and confusing navigation.

## Design Principles
- **Trust through transparency:** Clear order statuses, upfront pricing, and accessible support.
- **Professional execution:** The interface should feel like a serious tool for getting things done, not a casual social space.
- **Flexible yet structured:** Accommodate various service types without breaking the core workflow of request -> execute -> deliver.
- **Clear affordances:** Users should always know what their next possible action is (e.g., waiting for executor, action required).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard mobile accessibility practices (clear contrast, readable text sizes, touch targets at least 44x44pt). Ensure the chat and order tracking features are easy to navigate with screen readers.
