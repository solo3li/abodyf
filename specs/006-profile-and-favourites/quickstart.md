# Quickstart: User Profile & Favourites

## Overview
This feature allows users to manage their favorite services and customize their professional profile.

## Verification Steps

### 1. Favourites Flow
1. Browse services on the Home/Catalog screen.
2. Click the heart icon on a service card.
3. Navigate to the "Favourites" tab.
4. Verify the service appears in the list.
5. Search for the service using keywords in the Favourites search bar.
6. Click the heart icon again to remove it.

### 2. Profile Update Flow
1. Go to the "Profile" tab.
2. Click "Edit Profile".
3. Change your Name and Bio.
4. Click "Save".
5. Verify the changes are reflected on the profile screen.

### 3. Profile Picture Flow
1. In "Edit Profile", click the profile picture icon.
2. Select an image from your device.
3. Crop the image to a square when prompted.
4. Confirm upload.
5. Verify the new picture appears in the Profile and Chat headers.
6. Click "Remove Picture" to verify it reverts to the default placeholder.
